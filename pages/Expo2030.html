Riyadh is ready for an unprecedented Expo in 2030!


The Era of Change: Together for a foresighted tomorrow

The Kingdom of Saudi Arabia achieved victory in the bid to host World Expo 2030 in Riyadh, under the captivating theme, “The Era of Change: Together for a Foresighted Tomorrow.” Riyadh is poised to craft an unparalleled Expo edition that will etch an indelible mark on the hearts and minds of its global visitors.


What is Expo 2030?

Dating back to the mid-19th century, the World Expo serves as a platform for nations to showcase cultural, technological and economic achievements, and to promote cooperation, innovation and cultural exchange.

Why Expo 2030 Matters

Expo 2030 represents a unique opportunity to bring nations together to address the world's most pressing challenges. From technological advancements and environmental sustainability to cultural exchange and global cooperation, Expo 2030 will serve as a platform for innovation and collaboration. It will inspire solutions to global problems, create opportunities for future generations, and foster understanding among diverse communities. As we face a rapidly changing world, Expo 2030 will highlight the critical role that innovation and unity play in building a better future for all.


Future Goals of Expo 2030 for Saudi Arabia

  • Showcase Vision 2030: Highlight the country's ambitious transformation plan, focusing on economic diversification and reducing dependency on oil.
  • Promote Saudi innovation: Display the Kingdom's advancements in technology, sustainability, and smart cities.
  • Boost tourism and global presence: Attract millions of visitors, positioning Saudi Arabia as a global hub for culture and innovation.
  • Strengthen international partnerships: Foster cooperation with countries and organizations worldwide to drive shared goals.
  • Empower youth and future generations: Provide opportunities for Saudi youth to engage in global dialogues, innovation, and leadership development.
