Aspirational Strategy to Place the Kingdom on the Global Stage


1 Economic Sector

  • Investment in Renewable Energy: Harnessing solar and wind energy to power the future.
  • Boosting Local Industries: Encouraging manufacturing, technology, and innovation.
  • Expanding the Role of SMEs: Supporting small and medium enterprises to drive economic growth.

2 Education and Training

  • Implementing Digital Education: Enhancing online learning platforms to reach all students.
  • Promoting Vocational Training: Aligning education with labor market needs to prepare skilled professionals.
  • Encouraging Lifelong Learning: Instilling a culture of continuous development and education.

3 Culture and Entertainment

  • Expanding Cultural Festivals: Celebrating local arts, music, and traditions to promote tourism.
  • Developing Entertainment Venues: Building theaters, museums, and recreational facilities.
  • Encouraging International Events: Hosting global sports and cultural events to showcase Saudi Arabia.

4 Empowering Women

  • Supporting Women Entrepreneurs: Providing resources and funding to foster female-led businesses.
  • Increasing Workforce Participation: Encouraging women to join the workforce in diverse fields.
  • Promoting Leadership Roles: Ensuring women have equal opportunities in leadership positions.
