Saudi Vision 2030 is a strategic framework launched by Saudi Arabia to reduce its dependence on oil, diversify its economy, and develop public service sectors such as health, education, infrastructure, recreation, and tourism. It aims to position the Kingdom as a global investment powerhouse and foster innovation and sustainability.


Key Objectives

  • Diversifying the economy by encouraging investments in non-oil sectors.
  • Developing a vibrant society with strong roots in culture and heritage.
  • Improving the quality of life through enhanced healthcare, education, and housing.
  • Increasing the private sector's role in the economy.
  • Promoting tourism and entertainment to create more job opportunities.

Key Initiatives

  • Establishing the Public Investment Fund (PIF) to drive economic transformation.
  • Developing mega-projects like NEOM, a futuristic city that integrates technology and sustainability.
  • Promoting Saudi Arabia as a tourism destination through the Red Sea Project and other initiatives.
